# M5: verification against the original

The checks of the M5 spec (section 4), run against the original `dr.exe` under Wine as in [M4c](m4c.md), with `scripts/reference-run.sh --seed 1 --save 0:FILE --watch` and M3b's test game, the opponents driving (no `--no-ai`). Screenshots stay under `captures/`.

## The race's sounds

The recordings are compared with our sound rendered by `render-audio --startup --no-ai` (and `--cfg` for the runs with the music off), its keys taken from each run's memory: the mine key, Down, Up and the horn's flag frame by frame, the race over's box by when the frames stop, the help's keys by the frames stalling there (all 223 shots of the help run then equal our frames). Before the race the recordings and the game's clock part by some 2 s (in them the race starts 2.6 s later than ours, by the watch log's times 0.4 s), as M4b's tempo had shown, so each comparison is aligned on the race itself: `compare-audio` over the race's part, then the loudness every 20 to 250 ms around each sound, and `compare-audio` over the sound's own second.

- **The race's end call** (effect 5 on channel 2 when the race is over, `wreck`): with the music off it starts within 20 ms of the original's, its second is 0.12 dB louder, its octave bands within 1.9 dB, pitch 0 cents; with the music, every 100 ms of it within 2.6 dB. The fire's sound fading before it and the box's sounds after it are within 0.1 dB and 1 dB a quarter second.
- **The help's music** (the calmer order at half volume, `help`, both helps): every quarter second within 0.0 to 2.1 dB, ours some 0.8 dB louder throughout, as the race's music is; the second help's octave bands within 1.2 dB, pitch 0 cents. Its highest treble (the signal's second difference, some 35 dB under the music; above the octave bands, which match up to 11 kHz, and below 20 kHz) is 3 to 5 dB above the original's while the help plays its calmer order, and not in the race: not explained (FMOD's mixer is not ours), and too faint to hear.
- **The mines** (`effects-sound`, the music off): each drop where the original's is, within 1 to 3 dB over its 40 ms; the blast's second within 0.03 dB, its bands within 1.9 dB, pitch 0 cents.
- **The horn** (the same run): 2 dB over the idling engine in both; its second within 0.04 dB, its bands within 1.3 dB but 63 Hz, 3.5 dB louder in ours with the engine under it, pitch 0 cents.
- **Over the race with the music off:** the envelopes correlate 0.995 (`effects-sound`) and 0.990 (`wreck`), loudness per second within a median 0.08 and 0.05 dB, octave bands within 1.9 dB, pitch 0 cents; the race's first second, the original loading, differs. `race-effects-sound.sha256` holds the two renders with the music off.

## What the original does that the decompilations do not say

- **The results start black and fade in** (`postRaceMain` 0x42B709): the standings and the easy race's page drawn first, then 50 steps of 2 % up to 98 %; the medium and hard races' pages set the palette at 100 %. Escape after a page adds the remaining races' points without showing them.
- **Each place's car on a page has its own sixteen colours** (entries 64, 80, 224 and 240 on): `sub_424240` ramps its driver's colour there, eight steps up from a tenth and eight on towards white, the player's from `COPPER.PAL` and the others' from the cars' colours.
- **The races the player was not in are placed by car:** their entrants sorted by their cars (`sub_424510`, the original's unstable quicksort, in the sign-up itself), the best car first, so the computer's races have no result of their own.
- **The standings are sorted by points with the same unstable quicksort** (`sub_423C90`), then turned round (`sub_423E20`): drivers with equal points share a rank, and the player's place in the drivers' table (0x463CE8) moves with them. A saved game's player can therefore be any of the twenty drivers; DeadRally loaded only driver 19 before.
- **The statistics' race part is shown once:** the player's place (0x456BD8) is cleared after it, so the shop's statistics show only the first five rows. A best lap better than the circuit's record for the player's car, or the first lap on an empty record, becomes the record in `dr.cfg`'s table, the player's name upper-cased.
- **Loading the shop's pictures resets the menu's pulse** to 100 %, falling (`sub_41EE40` during "Please wait while loading..."), with the shop's loops: the pulsing colours after the results show it.
- **The way out depends on how the race was reached** (0x42BA03): with weapons on, the welcome shown and the shop's continue item seen (0x456B84, set whenever it is drawn selected), the screen stays as it is for 51 waits and the Underground Market the race was entered through then fades it out (0x4370C7), without its flag or the music's volume, which only an Escape from the market brings (0x456B60); otherwise the results fade out themselves, all but entries 96 to 127.
- **After each race the bottom panel tells the news** (`sub_4279C0`): its lines move up six, the last staying where it is, and a headline not told since all nineteen were told fills lines 17 to 20 in small B, `rand()` drawn until one is found.
- **The opponents' guide is read as unsigned bytes:** an opponent steers right when 16 less the guide under its right feeler, as a byte, is over 9 (so a guide above 16 wraps round and counts too), and left when 16 less the guide under its left feeler is under 7. DreeRally takes absolute values there.
- **The counter at 0x4A7E80**, past which an opponent would turn on its own, is never set: only the opponents' driving reads it, and every car starts it at 0.
- **The effect power-up's count runs down for every car** by the ticks between frames (0x41747B), and an opponent's steering wavers in 30-tick windows of it.
- **A race abandoned before its intro still runs the intro.** Escape held while the race loads opens the pause on the loop's first pass, before the intro, over the palette `setCircuitPalette` blacked out, so the box flies in and apart unseen. Abandoning (0x41770D) then jumps on to 0x417862 like the other answers, where the first pass calls the intro (`sub_404C30`); only after it does the loop end and the view tilt away.
- **The view's other way out (`sub_4055A0` from 0x405C40)** is taken when the HUD is not all in (0x456AA0 not 64). The race's set-up puts it in (`initRaceValues` 0x409AD7, with the bar's slide at 0x445010 at 256); only the status bar hidden with TAB (0x445028, 0x416D29) slides it out, 2 columns a tick (0x4174CB). M4c's record had the branch as a pause before the intro. After a first wait, the race's last frame (the buffer's 320 columns) is turned round its top left corner in squares of two pixels: a square at column `q`, row `r` of the squares shows the frame's at `(c(2q+1) >> 10) - (s(2r+1) >> 10)`, `(s(2q+1) >> 10) + (c(2r+1) >> 10)`, black unless inside the frame's outer rows and columns, with `c` and `s` the cosine and sine times 1024 of step 599 less (the factor's whole part less 8) of a table of 600 steps of 0.0104667 radians. The colours are the race's palette (not the shown one the intro left) over 150 as floats, times 150 less the factor; the sound's volume 436 a step of that. The factor starts at 8 and grows by 1.04 for each of the two ticks a frame takes while under 150: 38 frames, from just short of a whole turn to about a quarter turn back, the last cleared at once.
- **The gamepad in the race (`sub_4138A0` from 0x413A3E):** with the gamepad switched on (0x45EA00), the timer polls it every tick (0x43C650: each axis over 256, toward zero; buttons 1 to 4) into inputs 1 to 8 as Define Gamepad numbers them, the stick counting past 50 either side of a centre that is always 0 (0x417E80). Each of the seven controls' inputs in `dr.cfg` (0xB56, accelerate to mine) then adds its bit, after the mine key has taken the brake away: the pad's mine keeps a brake held with it (the horn's two bits) and is seen every tick it is held. No input is the horn's. A failed poll (no gamepad) switches the gamepad off (0x413AC6), as the menus' poll does (0x43C965); DeadRally reads nothing from a gamepad that is not connected but leaves the switch on, in the race as in the menus.
- **The rocket flames' picture (0x456AFC) is never set back:** only a flame's turn (0x40F651) writes it, so a race's flames go on from the last race's, through every game for as long as the program runs. DeadRally keeps it in the campaign (`Campaign::flame_phase`), which lasts as long. M4c's record also had the mines' slots' times kept from race to race; they are not: `initRaceValues` (0x409B13) sets every slot's state to a new mine's and its time to 0 for each race, and the race's count of mines (0x464F60) to 0, as DeadRally always did. Only the slots' places stay, which nothing reads past that count.

- **The race's other keys (0x416D13),** checked between F1 and the pass's drawing, after the pass's logic: TAB turns the status bar away or back once a press (its press kept in 0x46F200), and a wrecked player always has it back; F2 and F3 set the music's and the effects' volume to 0 or back to `dr.cfg`'s, F4 and F5 turn the scene's pictures (0x411B9A) and the shadows (0x417174) off and on, each key let go once seen; P (0x416E0C) silences channels 1 to 14, plays the help's calmer order at half volume and opens the pause's box with "game paused" in it (`racePauseMenu(0)`, where Y aborts nothing), then the order and the volume back and the engine again, and the pass is drawn. None of the switches is ever set back: the next race starts with them as they were left, the music or the effects silent from its start (0x4162D1), the status bar sliding away over the first frames, and the effects' volume stays off in the menus after the race, which only set the music's back.
- **The status bar slides after the frame is shown (0x417497):** the track's view (0x445010) widens 2 columns a tick of the frame up to 320 while the bar is away, and narrows 4 a tick back to 256; the HUD's width (0x456AA0) is the rest and the view's middle (0x445014) half the view. Everything follows them: the camera centres on the car across the wider view, the track is copied `(width >> 2) + 1` dwords a row, the scene's perspective and culling, the shadows, the cars and every sprite are drawn from the new left, and the pause's box lands half the HUD's width right of its place beside no HUD (0x40616F). With the bar fully away the HUD is not drawn at all (0x414110: only its clocks, and its medals stop rolling); while it is not all in, the small board (`SMALLBAR.BPK`, the player's place's, four on without weapons) is drawn first at the top left (0x413C90): the turbo bar, the weapons bar and the mines or the time, and the damage.
- **The time in a race without weapons (0x414B82):** the lap's clock, or for 210 ticks of the frames after each lap (9999 once finished) the lap's time, as minutes and two-digit seconds and hundredths (the ticks' remainder times 1.42) in the small font; the time shown is counted down by the HUD that shows it, and again by the small board, so while the bar is going or gone it runs out twice as fast; with the bar gone the hidden HUD counts it down a first time anyway.
- **The effect power-up's view (`sub_404730`):** while the player's count (0x4A8050) is above 0 when the frame goes onto the screen, it goes on wavering: the HUD's columns as they are, the view in 2x2 blocks each from up to 4 pixels across and 8 down or up, by a table of 3600 sines of 0.0175-radian steps times 1024 (built for each race, 0x4046C0) read with a phase that moves 3 a frame and is never set back (0x456AF4). The rows' wave starts from the phase itself rather than the phase in 1024ths. Blocks from beyond the view are black. Otherwise the frame is shown as it is and the player's count set to 0; then every car's count runs down by the ticks between the last two frames.
- **The pauses, the help and the race's end silence channels 1 to 14** (0x417583 and the like count from 0 to 13 and stop the count plus 1); DeadRally's Escape pause silenced 1 to 13, so a fourth car's horn on channel 14 sounded on through it.
- **After a pause the ticks between frames can count one fewer than DeadRally counts.** The original's timer counts its ticks on a thread of its own, and the pause sets that count back (0x406992); whether the tick that started the paused pass had already been counted by that pass's HUD depends on where the timer's thread falls within the frame. In the race-keys run's first try it had: the power-ups, which come by those ticks, came one frame later than DeadRally's after the box, so `rand()` drew for them a frame later. DeadRally always loses the tick, as the original does when its timer ticks after the frame's HUD, and leaves the rest to chance.
- **The spin rebuilds the effect's table (0x405E72)** with 600 steps of its own; the table is built afresh for each race and freed at its end, so a race's spin changes no other race's waves.

## Left for later
