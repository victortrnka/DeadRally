# M5: verification against the original

The checks of the M5 spec (section 4), run against the original `dr.exe` under Wine as in [M4c](m4c.md), with `scripts/reference-run.sh --seed 1 --save 0:FILE --watch` and M3b's test game, the opponents driving (no `--no-ai`). Screenshots stay under `captures/`.

## What the original does that the decompilations do not say

- **The opponents' guide is read as unsigned bytes:** an opponent steers right when 16 less the guide under its right feeler, as a byte, is over 9 (so a guide above 16 wraps round and counts too), and left when 16 less the guide under its left feeler is under 7. DreeRally takes absolute values there.
- **The counter at 0x4A7E80**, past which an opponent would turn on its own, is never set: only the opponents' driving reads it, and every car starts it at 0.
- **The effect power-up's count runs down for every car** by the ticks between frames (0x41747B), and an opponent's steering wavers in 30-tick windows of it.
- **A race abandoned before its intro still runs the intro.** Escape held while the race loads opens the pause on the loop's first pass, before the intro, over the palette `setCircuitPalette` blacked out, so the box flies in and apart unseen. Abandoning (0x41770D) then jumps on to 0x417862 like the other answers, where the first pass calls the intro (`sub_404C30`); only after it does the loop end and the view tilt away.
- **The view's other way out (`sub_4055A0` from 0x405C40)** is taken when the HUD is not all in (0x456AA0 not 64). The race's set-up puts it in (`initRaceValues` 0x409AD7, with the bar's slide at 0x445010 at 256); only the status bar hidden with TAB (0x445028, 0x416D29) slides it out, 2 columns a tick (0x4174CB). M4c's record had the branch as a pause before the intro. After a first wait, the race's last frame (the buffer's 320 columns) is turned round its top left corner in squares of two pixels: a square at column `q`, row `r` of the squares shows the frame's at `(c(2q+1) >> 10) - (s(2r+1) >> 10)`, `(s(2q+1) >> 10) + (c(2r+1) >> 10)`, black unless inside the frame's outer rows and columns, with `c` and `s` the cosine and sine times 1024 of step 599 less (the factor's whole part less 8) of a table of 600 steps of 0.0104667 radians. The colours are the race's palette (not the shown one the intro left) over 150 as floats, times 150 less the factor; the sound's volume 436 a step of that. The factor starts at 8 and grows by 1.04 for each of the two ticks a frame takes while under 150: 38 frames, from just short of a whole turn to about a quarter turn back, the last cleared at once.
