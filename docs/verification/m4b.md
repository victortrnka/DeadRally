## What the original does that the decompilations do not say

- **The race's intro** (`sub_404C30`, run on the race loop's first frame) tilts the view from 1 to 90 degrees, the step growing by 1.02 a tick from 0.9, the colours at the tilt's 90th; then 20 waits, the player's car flashing to 1.7 times its colours and back (6 and 26 steps), 50 waits, and the other colours coming back from grey in 32 steps of 8/256. The last steps stop at 248/256 (250/256 for the car), so the race's palette ends a little off the track's: (32, 49, 8) shows as (33, 50, 8).
- **Shadows darken only the cars:** `VARJO.TAB` maps entries 0 to 63, the cars' colours, two steps down a ramp and leaves the track's colours alone; the track's picture has its shadows drawn in.
- **The 3D scene's tables for kinds 0x81 to 0x83** are never filled; those triangles come out black. A second picture made for a triangle lands on the triangle before (`calculateSceTextureStructure` writes the slot before).
- **The race's sound** (0x416215): the menu's music stops, the track's `TRn-MUS.CMF` starts at the configured volume under a volume mask of 0, which the intro raises to the zoom's factor times 728 each frame; `GEN-EFE.CMF` holds the race's sounds: the engine (25 plus the car) at pitch 0x28000, the first light's sound (3, pitch 0x50000), the second's and the start's (44, pitches 0x20000 and 0x28000). The engine's pitch is set again every frame from the car's speed, unchanged while it stands.
- **A reversed circuit** (any circuit past 8, 0x432532) is its track turned half round (`calculateCircuitReversed` 0x40A9A0): the picture, the mask and the cars' sprites read backwards, the sprites' frames moved 48 round (each car's frame for a direction is the old frame for the opposite one), the pedestrians' sprites frame by frame, every place across (pedestrians, placed by their 16-square's corner, 17 less), the scene's points negated and its pictures' pixels reversed; the palette comes from `TRn-FLIP.PAL`, the power-ups' pictures from `OBST_REV.BPK`. A car's direction turns 48 steps round, but one short of it from the first half of the directions (`r + 47`). The scene's lights are worked out before the turn, from the unturned points.
- **The pause reads the keys held, not pressed.** `racePauseMenu` looks at `keysRead` once a frame before its wait, so a key pressed and released within one wait goes unseen: a scenario's `key y` (xdotool's 12 ms) was missed once; the scenarios hold their keys 100 ms (`keydown`, `keyup`), and our runs hold them 7 ticks.
- **The pause draws `rand()`** 100 times for where its 969 tiles start (columns below 316, along row 196) and twice a tile as they fly apart (a throw of `i / -25` up the box's middle plus 0 to 2, a push of -2 to 1); 19 tiles start a frame, each flying 70 frames, so the box lands 121 frames after Escape.
- **The race's start draws `rand()` 21 times** (`generateBigPowerUps` 0x409460): a value of 100 to 149 for each of 20 spots, and one time in four a big power-up (kind 7 or 8 at the track's 13th or 14th spot) painted into the track's picture from `OBSTACLE.BPK`.
- **The pedestrians' steps can come out of phase.** `sub_43C760` moves the timer on a tick at every call while it is behind the clock, and the pedestrians call it twice each when due. The race's music loads after the timer is set (0x41623F), so when the load takes longer than a tick, the first frame's pedestrians step a tick or two apart. Of four runs three were in phase, as DeadRally always is; the fourth was retaken.
- **The pedestrians step by the timer**, every 5 ticks, not by the race's frames: they move during the countdown.
