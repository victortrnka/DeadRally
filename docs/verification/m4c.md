# M4c: verification against the original

The checks of the M4 spec (section 4) for its third part, the player driving, run against the original `dr.exe` under Wine as in [M4b](m4b.md), with `scripts/reference-run.sh --seed 1 --save 0:FILE --no-ai` and M3b's test game. The scenarios hold keys (`keydown`, `keyup`); our runs hold them the ticks fitted to each run. Screenshots stay under `captures/`.

## The original's memory as the oracle

`scripts/reference-run.sh --watch` starts the original through `scripts/reference-watch.py`, which reads its memory (the game is its descendant, as `kernel.yama.ptrace_scope` 1 asks) and logs the race's frame counter, the cars and their handling each time the counter moves. `deadrally-headless trace` prints the same numbers for our run, and `scripts/compare-watch.py WATCH_LOG TRACE` shows the first frames where they differ; `--keys` lists the keys the original sampled for the player, frame by frame, so a run's key ticks no longer need fitting from screenshots. Nothing in the original is changed.

## What the original does that the decompilations do not say

- **The cars' handling comes from six tables** that `initParticipantValues` (0x401060) fills on its stack: engine, tires, steering, armour and armour upgrades by car and level, and each car's slew; the opponents are set up for the race's level, the player for a level of their own (3). DeadRally reads them by running that code over the player's `dr.exe`.
- **The movement (0x40BAB0) in the x87's 53 bits:** the speed eases by 2 % a tick and the accelerator, the brake, the turbo and the rocket push it as the original's constants say; the steering turns the car by its steering capacity a tick and slews it sideways; seven points round the car read the ground under it (walls below 4, slowing grounds, spinning ones, a boost, a random jolt); a wall under the car's sprite turns it 20 degrees away, then pushes it back, then shakes it loose with `rand()`. Degrees become radians through the original's slightly-off constant (0x4412B0).
- **The wheels (`sub_411D10`, every tick, every car):** at low speed with the accelerator held the wheels spin and shake the car by up to half a degree, two draws of `rand()` a tick; a skidding car paints marks through the track's `-SKI.TAB` into the track's picture (red ones through `-BLO.TAB` for a while after a pedestrian), throws a smoke puff off each rear wheel every 6 ticks (`SMOKE.BPK`), and the player's tires squeal on channel 6 once the start is given.
- **Every logic tick eases each car's push back and spin** (by 0.8696 and 0.8333, at the start of `sub_40CD10`).
- **The view runs ahead of the moving car** (`recalculateCircuitImageOffset` 0x40D560): 16 and 10.67 times its speed along its direction, a fifth of the way there each frame the target changes.
- **The keys are sampled by the timer** (`sub_4138A0`) into a ring of 16, and each pass of the race loop hands the player's car the samples since the last, oldest first; the arrows drive whatever `dr.cfg` sets, the turbo key also accelerates, and the first mine control counts once a press and takes the brake away.
- **The power-ups (`sub_410220`, once a pass):** after 350 ticks every pass tries the first 12 places in a random order, drawing `rand()` until each place has come up, and lays a power-up (by `rand() % 100`) where the place's own wait (100 to 149 ticks, then 300 to 499) is over, at most four at once; they blink out from their 1530th tick and go at the 2000th.
- **The first frame counts a tick.** Loading the race leaves the timer a tick behind, which the first frame catches up before its HUD, so the first `between` is 1: the power-ups' wait ends a pass earlier than 350 ticks after the loop's start, and the run's wheelspin only matches with that.
- **Only the player's car moves off the grid's sprite:** every car is stepped every tick, but the cars standing keep their direction (72 times 3.75 times the original's 0.2666... is 72 again in 53 bits).
- **The opponents' cars are set up for the game's difficulty** (0x456738, the licence's choice kept in `dr.cfg` and the saved game), not for the race chosen at the sign-up.
- **Laps (`checkVaiZones` 0x412DF0):** the track's `-VAI.BPK` numbers its zones on a grid of 4x4 pixels; a car moves on a zone when a front wheel reaches the next, completes a lap on the last, and its zone goes back to none when it stands on the last zone without having come round. A car ahead by zone with as many laps takes the place of one behind; the first car past its last lap ends the race for everyone at their line; wrecks drop to the last places in the order they were wrecked (`sub_413380`). Checked in [M5](m5.md): in the opponents' run every frame of the opponents' zones, laps (to their third) and places equals the original's memory; the race's end past the last lap is not yet seen there.
- **Cars knocking together (`sub_40CD10`):** two cars touch where their sprites overlap (the last such pixel row by row counts); each is pushed 0.7 times the difference of their moves; on each axis a car moving into the other is put back where it was (knocked back) and one that is not is only knocked; knocked back on the same axis, the two spin 2 degrees apart and step 0.6 pixels apart; meeting side-on, each spins by its moment over 4 (at most 3); a car with spiked wheels tears into the other with its front corners.
- **Picking a power-up up (`sub_410B90`, each car each logic tick):** a car takes a power-up when its middle is within 16 pixels and its sprite covers one of the four points round the power-up's middle: ammunition, turbo, money ($ by the race and the player's rank: $50 in the first race, up to $500; $400 in the Adversary's race), an effect on the view for 560 ticks, a repair of 2 to 5 % (`rand()`) or 20 %, a bonus, big money; the pixels go back, a note floats up from the place (`sub_410050`: `$` and the money, the repair in percent), the next power-up waits 240 ticks and the place 200 to 349 (`rand()`).
- **The opponents' engines are balanced to the player (`balanceIAEngineInRace` 0x40B920, once a pass):** an opponent a zone behind gets 7 to 18 % more by the difficulty, two or more behind 12 to 32 %; one ahead loses 6 to 12 % and two ahead 3 to 6 %, except in the third race of the sign-up.
- **A pedestrian run over (`sub_410FA0`, each car each logic tick):** within 20 pixels of the pedestrian's middle with a pixel of the car's sprite over it: the pedestrian lies dead, the car loses three times its armour short of 1024, is jolted up to 3 pixels each way and spun -5 to 4 degrees (`rand()`), keeps a tenth of its speed backwards, leaves red tracks for 45 ticks, and a scream (one of three by `rand()`) plays on channel 3 as loud as the car is near the player's.
- **The machine guns (`sub_40E180`, each car each logic tick from the race's 431st frame):** each car type has one or two guns (an angle off the car, a reach, a flash's kind; read from `initParticipantValues`), the next gun taking its turn every frame (`sub_40E960`); a shot leaves the gun's muzzle with a spread of -3 to 2 (`rand()`) on each axis and is followed 130 pixels in steps of 5: a car whose sprite it meets loses its armour short of 1024 times the shooter's car's factor (0.2 to 0.95), a wall in the mask stops it, and at each step one pedestrian in five from a random one (`rand()`) may fall. A car standing still turns on the spot with its steering keys. The flashes are `FLAME1.BPK` to `FLAME6.BPK` by the gun's kind, 48 directions; the sparks `SHOTS.BPK`, drawn for three frames after the hit (`sub_40EBC0`).
- **Mines (`sub_40F6A0`, each car each logic tick from the race's 431st frame):** the mine key (not with the brake) drops one 1.9 car sizes behind the car, both offsets rounded half up, unless another lies within 8 pixels; the car then waits 50 ticks, even when the mine was not dropped. A mine is painted into the track's picture (`MINES1A.BPK`); a car whose sprite covers an armed one sets it off: the crater (the last of `BLOWI.BPK`'s six pictures) is painted in, the car loses twenty times its armour short of 1024, is pushed up to half a pixel each way, jolted up to 5 pixels, spun -10 to 11 degrees (`rand()`) and keeps 0.7 of its speed backwards. The blast is drawn after the cars (`sub_40FE20`), a picture each 5 ticks of the timer; then the mine leaves the list and the ones after it move down, their times staying in their slots. The HUD counts the mines the car has left.
- **The horn (`sub_413500`, once a pass from the start):** the brake and the mine key held together (the eighth control holds both) sound the car's horn on channel 11 plus its place, at a pitch of its own; the two keys then do nothing else. The player's horn is at full volume, another's 88 less a pixel away; it stops when let go or when the car has finished.
- **The rocket's flame (`sub_40F450`, each car after the guns' flashes):** a car whose rocket burned since the last frame, with turbo left, shows a flame 2.3 car sizes behind it (`ROCKET1.BPK` and `ROCKET2.BPK`, 24 directions); one phase for all cars picks the picture and turns when a flame is drawn 4 ticks of the timer after that car's last turn; the burn is forgotten every frame. The phase is never reset, so a later race starts with the last one's, as DeadRally's now does ([M5](m5.md)).
\n
- **A wreck burns (`drawCarInRace` 0x40D920, `sub_43AFC0`):** over a car's sprite with nothing left of its damage bar, the eight pictures of `BURN1A.BPK` one after another, the next 3 ticks of the timer after the last move; the player's always, another's when near the view. The fire's time is the same field as the rocket flame's (0x4A7EE0). There is no other explosion: `BLOWI.BPK` is the mines'.
- **The HUD's medals roll (`drawLeftRaceBar_414220` from 0x4147B0):** a board whose place changed rolls its medal through the pictures between the old place and the new (7 a place), half a picture a tick for each place it moves, and stops at the new place's; a wrecked leader's medal takes 14 frames to reach 4th.
- **The race's end (0x416A38, 0x4172A7):** each tick the counter at 0x4AA508 goes up once while the player has finished or is wrecked, and once more while every car but one stands (0.5 or slower) finished or wrecked. Past 300, every channel from 1 to 14 is silenced, effect 5 is asked for on channel 2 (checked against a recording in [M5](m5.md)), and the pause's box flies in saying the race is over and waits for Enter; when it has flown apart the race's last frame shows for a tick, then the view tilts away.
- **The view tilting away (`sub_4055A0`):** the intro's zoom the other way, from 90 down to 1 with the same steps: the race's palette over 90 (the intro's, so the first frame can be a step darker than what was shown), the HUD sliding out to the left with the columns it leaves cleared, the track's view turned upside down and drawn from its bottom (so its first frame shows the view a row lower under a black row), the sound fading with it; the screen is cleared after the last frame. With the HUD not all in (the status bar hidden with TAB) another branch spins the frame away instead ([M5](m5.md)).
- **Abandoning the race (`sub_413300`):** the player swaps places with each car still racing behind them, in the cars' order, and so ends behind them all.
- **The help (`keyMenuInRace` 0x407330, F1 between a pass's logic and its drawing, 0x416B21):** every channel from 1 to 14 is silenced and the track's song jumps to a calmer order (0x37 for `TR1` and `TR5`, 0x2D for `TR2` and `TR4`, 0x32 for `TR3` and `TR6` to `TR9`) at half volume. The race fades to black, a 63rd of each colour a tick in 16.16; the keys' page (`KEYCOM3.BPK`) fades in with the global keys, the eight controls' keys and seven gamepad inputs in the small font, named from the help's own tables (dot-padded, and unlike the menu's without a skip after 0xCB); it waits for a key pressed once it is up, fades out, and the tips' page (`INFO2.BPK`) does the same. Then the race fades back in to the race's palette, a step brighter than the intro left it; the song goes back to its order at full volume, the engine starts again and the pass is drawn. In the pause, F1 ends the box and leaves F1 held, so the next pass opens the help.

## Left for later

- **A lap against the original:** done in [M5](m5.md), by the opponents' driving: their laps and places equal the original's memory frame by frame in the opponents' run.
- **The HUD's last lap time in races without weapons** (`drawLeftRaceBar_414220` from 0x414B82: the lap's time for 210 ticks after each lap), with the laps.
- **The race's results** after the view has tilted away, and what they read: the places, the money picked up, the bonus power-up's flag (0x4A7AAC); lap records kept for the session.
- **The effect power-up's view** (kind 4): the player's view drawn wavering by `sub_404730` while its 560 ticks run down by the ticks between frames; DeadRally counts nothing yet, so the power-up only plays its call. With the power-ups' other effects in M5.
- **The race's other keys** the help lists: TAB (the status bar), F2 and F3 (music and effects on and off), F4 and F5 (textures and shadows), P (the "game paused" box over the calmer music).
- **The gamepad's part of the keys' sampling** (`sub_4138A0`): done in [M5](m5.md), from the binary; no run of the original, whose runner has no gamepad.
- **Two things the original keeps from race to race:** done in [M5](m5.md). The rocket flames' phase (0x456AFC) is kept, now in DeadRally too, checked in the original's memory over two races; the mines' slots' times are not kept (`initRaceValues` sets them for each race), as DeadRally already did.
- **The tilt's other branch:** done in [M5](m5.md). It is the end with the status bar hidden (TAB), not a race abandoned in a pause before the intro; such a race runs the intro and then tilts away as usual, which DeadRally now does too.
- **Sounds not yet checked by ear:** the race's end call, the help's music, the horns, the mines: measured against recordings of the original in [M5](m5.md), all where and as loud as the original's; nobody has listened to them yet.
