# M6: verification against the original

The checks of the M6 spec (section 4), run against the original `dr.exe` under Wine as in [M5](m5.md), with `scripts/reference-run.sh --seed 1 --save 0:FILE` and the test game of M3b changed for the run (a copy whose player leads on points: `captures/leader.sg`, made by the scratchpad's `arm-save.py points=150`; `leader_save` in `crates/headless/tests/menu_run.rs` makes the same file). Screenshots stay under `captures/`.

## What the original does that the decompilations do not say

- **The welcome box** (0x41788D) flies in after the intro of a new game's first race (0x464F44, set by a new game, cleared by the race's books at 0x433733, by every load at 0x422362, 0x42F536 and 0x439E41, and by the game's end at 0x439F77). It is `racePauseMenu` with the box's nine lines, but Y does not abort the race there (0x464F68 is not -1) and F1 opens the help after it. Every box of `racePauseMenu` flies in over the race's buffer (0x464F14 from column 96), not the screen: after the intro the screen's last column is still black.

- **The Adversary's screen waits 480 times at most** (0x435496) and then starts the race in the Arena by itself, as Enter does; any other key does nothing. A run of the original that missed its Escape (`captures/adversary-timeout`) went into the race after them.
- **Escape on it fills every race with the others and draws four places for the cars** before `postRaceMain(1)` (0x4356D0, 0x4356F3); the race last entered (or the first, in a game loaded since the program started) takes those places. After the results the sabotage is on sale again unless the player leads.
- **The results first making the player the leader** (0x456BC4 remembers the last results; a loaded game starts without it) fade every entry out, whichever way out they take, and play `ENDANI0.HAF` under `TR0-MUS.CMF` with `ENDANI0E.CMF` (`openAnimation`); then the menus' music, and the palette composed with the title's entries 96 to 127 lit.
- **The end** (`showEndAnim_4312D0`): `ENDANI.HAF`, then the title faded in (0 to 96 %, 4 % a wait) and out (100 % to 0), the best ten with the winner put in before the first entry with more races (`dr.cfg` written; a border with `CHOO2`'s corners cut to 12 rows, `drawBorder_421980`, round the row), the panel telling of the win (`sub_427BC0`, 0x456618), faded in, a key, and the way out with the music falling; then a new game's menus.

## The race in the Arena (M6b)

The runs go on from `adversary`'s way in: the leader's test game, the shop, the Underground Market and its way on, Return on the Adversary's screen, the preview and the race, `--watch` without `--no-ai` (the Adversary driving). The original's race is compared frame by frame with `trace` (`scripts/compare-watch.py`), now also on the gun each car fires next (`ag`, 0x4A68E0), the ticks between the HUD's last two frames (`bt`, 0x4A9EA4) and the ticks before the next power-up (`pw`, 0x456AC4).

### What the original does there

- **Enter on the Adversary's screen** (0x4354C1) sets the race to 3 (`[0x462CE8]`), the player's place on the grid to the second (`[0x45FC20]` 1) and the race's money and prize to 0, then calls `previewRaceScreen(2)`: two cars.
- **The preview** (0x4327CE, 0x43285C, 0x432AF9): `TSHAPE19.BPK` at (264, 100), the track `TR0`, 9 laps, never turned round; "PRIZE MONEY: " with the Arena's own word (0x444064) instead of a price. Both places of the grid hold the player's record; the first shows the Adversary's name (0x444058, upper-cased) and face (`FACEXX.BPK`, 0x45FA7C) and no rank, the second the player's rank, name and face. With fewer than four cars the empty places are hatched in colour 0xC4, every other pixel, a pixel further right on odd rows: the fourth's 100x91 from (131, 261), the third's from (19, 222) (0x43235E, 0x4323BC). No opponent's weapons are drawn (both entrants are the player), so the preview draws no `rand()`.
- **The Adversary is set up whenever the player has more points than the first drivers of the standings, as many as there are cars** (0x433241), not than everyone; the standings keep the drivers sorted, so in a game it is the player leading. The first racer becomes the Adversary (0x433285): the name at 0x44404C, the game's difficulty as its level, no damage, no rocket, spiked wheels, eight mines with weapons on, car 6; the money power-ups are worth $400 by the same check. The colours of the third and fourth racers become `CARCOL.PAL`'s entry 10 (0x4332EE), which in the Arena colour the ramps of the two empty places.
- **Car 6** (0x401FBC, after the tables have done every car): the engine 4.5, 4.6 or 4.7 by the difficulty with weapons on in the player's race, 4.3, 4.4 or 4.5 without; the Pickup's steering and the Lotus's armour with every upgrade, both at the second car's level (the player's, 3), the steering slowed by the second car's engine upgrade; no slide from the tires (0); size 10.5; two guns 30 degrees either side, reach 19, flash 4; a hit from them takes 0.95 of the target's armour short of 1024. The race leaves the first ramp (entries 15 to 24) as the track has it when the first car is car 6 (0x409FCF); the sprites are `SPECIAL.BPK`, spiked or not. The balance of the engines is off (0x416AF7) and nobody hears they are lapped (0x413274).
- **The HUD hatches the boards of the places without a car** (0x4143AE): black, every other pixel, a pixel further right on odd rows.
- **The Arena's lap records are circuit 0's**: the race's record (0x433325) and the statistics' (0x4251DF) are read at the circuit after the three races' (0x46126F), a byte nothing ever writes. The statistics show "best lap ever" from circuit 0's record for the player's car, and a better lap in the Arena becomes circuit 0's record in `dr.cfg`.
- **The books** (0x4334F7): the player's car is the second, so their place, their money power-ups ($400 each, nothing else: 0x433D0F), their damage carried over (0x4344CF) are the second car's. The races' last incomes are cleared for the four entrants (0x43375A), of which the Arena's third and fourth are the bytes of a picture's address left in the entrants' table (0x45EB5E, 0x45EB5F): whichever drivers those bytes name lose a last income no screen shows. DeadRally leaves that out.
- **After the race, a player who leads** (0x434737): the next three races drawn (`calculateNextRaces`), filled at once (`addParticipantToRace(1)` until full, at least once), and a place 1 to 4 drawn for each of four cars, none twice (the results use none of them: no page is the Arena's); then, the race won (place 1), the end (0x4347CB), without bringing the menus' sound back first (0x4345BA); otherwise the results (`postRaceMain(0)`), whose pages are those three races.
- **Which gun fires goes by the frames drawn**: the gun firing next (0x4A68E0) moves on at every frame drawn (0x40EB89), so a frame the original's loop takes two ticks over fires the other gun from then on, and `rand()`, which passes draw (the power-ups' wait, 0x456AC4), parts from DeadRally's. The runs are made on an idle machine; one made while DeadRally was being built took two ticks over 189 frames and parted at the Adversary's first shots.
