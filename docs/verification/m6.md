# M6: verification against the original

The checks of the M6 spec (section 4), run against the original `dr.exe` under Wine as in [M5](m5.md), with `scripts/reference-run.sh --seed 1 --save 0:FILE` and the test game of M3b changed for the run (a copy whose player leads on points: `captures/leader.sg`, made by the scratchpad's `arm-save.py points=150`; `leader_save` in `crates/headless/tests/menu_run.rs` makes the same file). Screenshots stay under `captures/`.

## Results

| Check | Scenario | Result |
|---|---|---|
| The Adversary's screen and Escape | `adversary`, seed 1, the leader's test game: the shop, the Underground Market, its way on, Escape on the Adversary's screen, Return on each results screen; a shot every 200 ms from the way on | **Pass.** 98 of the 100 shots equal our frames: the Adversary's screen wiped in over the market (the title, the Adversary, the box saying Escape backs out, the border), the screen and the music fading after Escape, the three races' results with the races filled and the cars' places drawn, the statistics, and the screen kept after the last key. s000 and s001, as the screen wiped in, differ in the pulsing colours of the Adversary's picture only (the pulse's phase on the way into the market). |

## What the original does that the decompilations do not say

- **The Adversary's screen waits 480 times at most** (0x435496) and then starts the race in the Arena by itself, as Enter does; any other key does nothing. A run of the original that missed its Escape (`captures/adversary-timeout`) went into the race after them.
- **Escape on it fills every race with the others and draws four places for the cars** before `postRaceMain(1)` (0x4356D0, 0x4356F3); the race last entered (or the first, in a game loaded since the program started) takes those places. After the results the sabotage is on sale again unless the player leads.
- **The results first making the player the leader** (0x456BC4 remembers the last results; a loaded game starts without it) fade every entry out, whichever way out they take, and play `ENDANI0.HAF` under `TR0-MUS.CMF` with `ENDANI0E.CMF` (`openAnimation`); then the menus' music, and the palette composed with the title's entries 96 to 127 lit.
- **The end** (`showEndAnim_4312D0`): `ENDANI.HAF`, then the title faded in (0 to 96 %, 4 % a wait) and out (100 % to 0), the best ten with the winner put in before the first entry with more races (`dr.cfg` written; a border with `CHOO2`'s corners cut to 12 rows, `drawBorder_421980`, round the row), the panel telling of the win (`sub_427BC0`, 0x456618), faded in, a key, and the way out with the music falling; then a new game's menus.
