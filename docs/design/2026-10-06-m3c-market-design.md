# M3c — Buying, the Underground Market and the loan shark: design

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** written without the owner, who asked to go on milestone after milestone without stopping; section 2 lists the decisions taken on their behalf; [docs/verification/m3c.md](../../verification/m3c.md) records the checks against the original.
- **Scope:** the last part of milestone M3: the shop's purchases (engine, tire and armour upgrades, repairs, a new car with its paint), the Underground Market with its weapons and the loan shark, the sabotage and the offers after a sign-up, and the quick save and load keys.
- **Builds on:** [M3a](2026-10-06-m3a-new-game-design.md), [M3b](2026-10-06-m3b-saves-shop-design.md).

## 1. Goal

Everything the player can buy between races works as in the original: the same prices, refunds, messages and animations, the same money afterwards, pixel for pixel, and a game saved after shopping is the original's file byte for byte.

## 2. Decisions taken on the owner's behalf

1. **M3c comes in parts on one branch:** the shop's purchases first, then the Underground Market and the loan shark, then the hitman's offer. *Cost if wrong:* none; the order of work only.
2. **The final race against the Adversary** (the player ahead of every other driver on points when going on from the shop) leads to the sign-up until M6 brings that race. *Cost if wrong:* none until then; points cannot change before races exist.
3. **The statistics and the shop's cheat words come with the race results (M5).** "See current statistics" runs the results screen's code (`postRaceMain(2)`), and two of the four cheat words re-sort the drivers by points (0x423C90, 0x423E20), which moves the player's record as the results after a race do. Until then the Start Racing menu's statistics row does nothing and the shop takes no cheat words. *Cost if wrong:* a menu row and four cheats missing until M5.
4. **The sabotage's clock.** The original seeds `rand()` again from `SDL_GetTicks()` when the sabotage strikes (0x42DEE1); DeadRally counts that clock from the startup's seed at 14 ms a tick, and the reference runner can fix the original's (`--sabotage-clock`). *Cost if wrong:* none for a player; the damage is a draw either way.
5. **A damaged or foreign quicksave is not found**, as a slot holding one counts as empty (M3b decision 2); the original would load it. *Cost if wrong:* none known.

## 3. Facts about the original

- **Upgrades** (`enterShop` 0x4373B0, engine 0x43805F): the price checked against the money (`hasInsuficientMoneyToBuy` 0x421E50 writes how much is missing under the popup's title and sounds effect 23 on channel 2), then paid and added to the car's worth; the box shows the next level (or the "no more" picture) with its price and what was bought (`reloadEngineAnimation` 0x4212F0 and the two after it, texts 240 bytes a level from 0x450DB8, 0x451538, 0x451CB8); 310 shop passes later the item's description comes back (`framesToWaitAfterBuy` 0x456B70, car and upgrades only).
- **Repairs** (0x438383): ten points of damage, what is left under ten, at the price the repair box shows (computed in another order than the box's, so the two can differ by a few dollars under 10 % damage); paid and added to the car's worth; effect 31.
- **A new car** (0x4374A5): the refund is a quarter of the car's worth rounded up, less the damage's repair, never negative, rounded down to tens; the popup offers the car for the price less the refund (or returns the difference when the refund is more), "yes" selected; the car turns and the cursor beside the answer turns, two waits a pass. "Yes" trades the car in (upgrades and damage gone), redraws every box and asks for the paint: Left and Right move the colour by 2 (0 to 254), two waits a pass; Enter ends it, the car box moves to the next car and says what was bought (`showCarBought` 0x4210C0).
- **Continue** (0x4384BE): a wrecked car (100 % damage) cannot go on without weapons; with weapons the Underground Market (0x436700) comes first; without, effect 24 and the sign-up, or the Adversary when the player leads every other driver on points.
- **The Underground Market** (`enterBlackMarketScreen` 0x436700): the shop fades out over 51 waits with its music (palette entries 0–95 and 128–255, the continue flag turning every other wait), the market's music starts at order 0x31, the market is drawn (`drawBlackMarketScreen` 0x4237A0: the title, the side panel, the loan shark, the four weapons with their prices, or sold out, and the way on) and fades in over 50 waits, the first visit with its popup. Each pass two waits; Up and Down move between the mines and the loan shark, Left and Right along the row (effect 26). A weapon in stock and affordable is paid for (effect 28), fitted (8 mines, the others once) and sold out; the loan shark lends by car or takes his money back (effect 29); refusals sound effect 23 on channel 2. The way on sounds effect 24 and opens the sign-up; Escape fades the market out and the shop back in (`postLoadedOrLicense` from 0x438960), its music's order restored.

- **After the sign-up** (0x435F88): `sabotageScreen` (0x42DD10) repairs every other car; with the sabotage bought, weapons on and the player not leading, the best-ranked rival in the player's race starts 25 to 49 % damaged (`rand()` seeded again from the clock) and a popup says so, waiting for a key. Otherwise, at the hitman's chance (5 %, 2 % more after each sign-up he misses, up to 97 %) with weapons on, a coin decides between the drug dealer and the hitman (0x431B30): his picture, his lines, a victim drawn from the player's race for the hitman, the pay by the player's car; after 50 waits his voice (effect 5 on channel 5), after 20 more the question, "yes" selected, Escape ignored. Without either the screen lingers. The race follows.
- **Quick save and load** (`sub_4221A0`, each pass of the shop and the market): F2 held writes `DR.SG7` under the quicksave's name, F3 held reads it back; the screen is drawn afresh at full brightness with the selected item's border and a confirmation ("saved", "loaded" or "not found") over it, which goes when a key ends it. A tap shorter than a pass is not seen.

## 4. Verification

The test game of M3b, loaded into the original (`reference-run.sh --save 0:FILE --seed 1`), goes through every purchase: an engine and a tire upgrade to their maximum, two armour upgrades, the repair down to nothing, a car too dear, and a cheaper car declined, then bought and painted. A second run goes into the Underground Market: the loan shark's loan and its paying back, every weapon, back to the shop, and on to the sign-up. Three more runs show the sabotage's popup after a sign-up, the hitman's offer (a seed where he comes) with both answers selected, and quick saves and loads in the shop and the market. Every shot must equal one of our frames, and the quicksave the original writes must equal ours byte for byte.

## 5. Tests

Data-free: an upgrade is paid for and adds to the car's worth; short of money nothing changes; mines are paid for, fitted and then sold out; a car full of mines finds them sold out; the loan shark lends by car and is paid back, and lends nothing for a Vagabond; the sabotage damages one rival by 25 to 49 %; an offer waits for its answer and Escape gives none; F2 held saves into the quicksave slot and F3 loads it back, or finds nothing. Data tests run the scenarios' keys (manifests `shop-purchases-run.sha256`, `market-run.sha256`, `sabotage-run.sha256`, `offer-run.sha256`, `quick-save-run.sha256`) and pin the market's prices.
